# Research Index

One-line map of every research artifact in this repo: the question it answers, the path, what's inside, and how fresh it is. Start here when hunting for research; add a line whenever a new research artifact lands. House-rule precedence applies everywhere — where research conflicts with CLAUDE.md or MEMORIES.md, the house rules win.

## SOQ writing

- **How do I write an SOQ in Uriah's voice?** — `SOQ Research/soq-writing-guide.md` — target voice, anti-AI checklist, STAR structure, the ask-then-draft process. REQUIRED reading before drafting any SOQ (2026-06, maintained).
- **What goes in the SOQ header?** — `SOQ Research/soq-header-formatting.md` — title + name/JC line placement, sourced to CalHR's own guidance and templates (2026-06-18).
- **How are SOQs and 678s actually scored?** — `SOQ Research/std678-soq-deep-research-2026-07.md` — five-agent research pass against official sources (CalHR HR Manual, SPB, SCO, CDT); scoring machinery + 2024-2026 changes. Complements, does not supersede, the field guide (2026-07-08).

## STD 678 and hiring process

- **How does CA state hiring work end to end?** — `Hiring Research/CA_State_Hiring_Field_Guide.docx` — exams, eligibility, SOQs, the 678; mirror-the-duty-statement, spell-out-acronyms, never "see resume" (2026-06).
- **What do state workers and hiring managers say firsthand?** — `Hiring Research/Reddit Corpus/CA-State-Job-Guide.md` — synthesis of 313 archived r/CAStateWorkers threads: screener DQ statistics, worked interview rubric, post-interview signals, timelines, salary placement. Freshness: through 2026-07-09 (re-harvested 2026-07-11); only as fresh as the last harvest — re-harvest from a Claude Code session. RESEARCH, not policy.
