## The 3 things a panel will most likely probe

The duty statement puts 25% on supporting Teams, Zoom, and conference-room hardware (hubs, cameras, audio, recording, event dry-runs). You have no professional AV / conference-room support history, and the SOQ/resume deliberately did not claim any.
- **How to answer (honest, fast-ramp):** "I haven't run conference-room AV professionally, so I'd treat it as the first thing I get up to speed on. I'm comfortable in Windows and Microsoft 365 and I pick up new tools quickly, and the core of AV support is the same troubleshooting and customer-service approach I already use — get the meeting working, keep the user calm, document the setup so it's repeatable." Don't fake familiarity with specific AV gear.

### 2. IT asset / inventory management (25% deployment + 10% receiving into the asset system) — real gap
The role tracks hardware in an asset management system and validates received equipment. You have no professional asset-tracking/inventory experience (this is the same gap that made us pass on JC-521906).

I'd learn their system quickly and keep the inventory accurate." Don't claim a specific asset tool.

### 3. Depth of real-world / enterprise experience (early-career profile)
Your hands-on IT is a ~4-month internship plus WGU lab work, a homelab, and non-IT customer service. A panel comparing you to people with desk tenure will test how real your skills are.
