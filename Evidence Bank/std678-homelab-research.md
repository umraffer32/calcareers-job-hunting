Verified via a deep-research pass on 2026-06-22 against primary CalHR/CalCareers sources. **Decision: keep the STD 678 Employment History grid to real paid/volunteer JOBS only (internship, Block Advisors, Robert's). Do NOT add a "Self-Directed IT/Network Lab (HomeLab)" entry or an "Academic Coursework / WGU" entry to the 678 employment grid.** The homelab and coursework belong in the SOQ (the scored competitive instrument) and the resume Projects + "Technical Support and Lab Experience" sections, which is where they already live. **Why:** (1) the STD 678 names "paid, military and/or volunteer" experience for that section, and a solo homelab has no employer/verifier so it does not fit; (2) CalHR HR Manual 3002 only credits experience performed as a routine "position," and explicitly EXCLUDES experience that is part of the educational curriculum, so WGU coursework miscast as a job earns nothing and is redundant with the Education section; (3) CalHR 3004 + Caltrans/CDCR SOQ guidance make the SOQ the sanctioned home for projects/coursework/non-paid work; (4) Uriah's MQ is already met by the WGU degree, and ITA/ITS I are 100% Training-and-Experience exams scored via the SOQ, so homelab/coursework earn competitive points THERE, not as fake employment time; (5) the 678 is signed under penalty of perjury, so inflating a homelab into a "Network Administrator (HomeLab)" job title (as the older JC-501379 678 did) is exactly the overstatement to avoid. **This REVERSES an in-session lean I gave earlier the same day** (I had recommended reinstating the two entries; that was overconfident judgment, not verified — Uriah called for the research and it corrected me). Do not re-suggest adding homelab/coursework to the 678 grid. The real lever for "the 678 should address the desirable quals better" is a duty-statement-mirroring SOQ plus tight duty-mirroring Duties blocks on the REAL jobs, plus the degree for the MQ. **Honesty facts surfaced while drafting the (now-scrapped) homelab entry, pin for the record:** Uriah does NOT own physical Cisco enterprise gear (the old JC-501379 678 claim was dishonest — never reuse it); his IOS / command-line-interface experience is via Cisco Packet Tracer (the WGU capstone was a Packet-Tracer network design), NOT bare metal; he DOES have a real uninterruptible power supply (UPS) connected to his mini PC servers (power protection/safe shutdown, not "outage simulation"); the ~2.5 years of homelab work is defensible though GitHub documentation only started in the last few months.
