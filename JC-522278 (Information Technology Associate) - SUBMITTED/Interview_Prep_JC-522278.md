Lead with the troubleshooting discipline and the documentation habit, not a claim of desk tenure. ### 2. ServiceNow / ITSM tool (named in the duties)

The internship was fully remote, so no end-user hardware support there. Frame it as comfortable troubleshooting hardware to root cause with a fast ramp on their workstation, printer, and mobile fleet. Do NOT claim professional printer / mobile / MDM support you lack.
