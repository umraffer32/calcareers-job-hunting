### 2026-06-17 — Settled: how to handle a "lead/senior" gap in an SOQ
When applying up to a lead/senior role Uriah has no formal authority for, do NOT write a disclaimer ("I have not led before"). The SOQ is a screening instrument often scored by non-technical HR against a rubric, so a stated negative is a documented reason to deduct. Instead, address the lead dimension affirmatively: name that you understand what the role adds, then show the leadership-adjacent habits he genuinely has (documentation others can follow = mentoring muscle, stepping up to cover, coordinating with partner teams/vendors, prioritizing a queue by impact). Candor about the gap belongs at the interview, not the SOQ. Applied this on JC-521544.
